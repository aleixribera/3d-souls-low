# Research, 2026-11-20

First research file. One question, kept short because the network dropped mid-search (github.com and api.github.com
stopped resolving); what is unverified says so.

## The question

**Can the loop turn reference video into Blender animation for free, on this PC, well enough for boss attacks?**
It decides next week: PLAN item 5 says hand-keyed animation is the weak link, and this review's blind panel (5 of 5
judges) named the same thing as the biggest giveaway: "stiff, upright bodies standing still", "no motion blur, impact
VFX or camera energy".

## Findings

1. **This PC can run single-camera motion capture locally.** It has an NVIDIA RTX 5060 with 8 GB (`nvidia-smi`).
   The monocular research models need a CUDA GPU; 8 GB is enough for inference on short clips (training is not
   needed). Nothing has to leave the PC or go through an account.
2. **GVHMR** (zju3dv/GVHMR, SIGGRAPH Asia 2024, TPAMI 2026; 2.3k stars, pushed 2026-09-15): one video in, a
   world-grounded human body motion out (SMPL parameters), with a Colab demo. Checked through the GitHub API before it
   dropped. **Unverified:** its licence is a custom one (GitHub reports `NOASSERTION`); ZJU's 3D vision group has used
   non-commercial academic terms before. The game is free and played among friends, but read the LICENSE before use.
   No official Blender path: SMPL to a Blender armature needs the SMPL-X Blender add-on or a retarget script, then a
   retarget onto our rigs.
3. **FreeMoCap** (AGPL-3.0, pushed 2026-11-14, active): multi-camera first, built for people recording themselves;
   a poor fit for single game clips. Pose2Sim is similar (several cameras).
4. **SyncRig** (Apache 2.0): a Blender add-on taking video or webcam. Its pose model, and whether it gives real 3D
   from one camera, are unverified (the page didn't load).

## What it changes

- Item 5 should try GVHMR first: run it on two or three reference attack clips (Sekiro, Lies of P), retarget onto the
  Pillager Captain's armature in Blender, then keep the keys and exaggerate them by hand (anime timing: holds, smears,
  sharper key poses). Raw mocap is realistic, not anime: it is a base, not the result.
- One timed pass decides it: if one attack goes from clip to the Captain's rig in under about 2 hours with a judge
  scoring it above the hand-keyed version, it becomes part of the boss system. If not, fall back to a pose library
  and hand keys, and say so in `BACKLOG.md`.
- Not settled this week: cel shading at 60 fps on integrated graphics (PLAN item 4). The three.js advice found agrees
  on one point: for permanent toon outlines use the per-material `OutlineEffect` (inverted hull), not the screen-space
  `OutlinePass`, which costs a full-screen pass and was reported to halve frame rates on large scenes. No integrated-GPU
  numbers were found; item 4's own frame-time check is the measurement.
