# STORY: The Hunger That Wears a King

The one story of the game, merged from Ashmere's road and the old second road and rebuilt as a single tale of
fourteen chapters in three acts. This is the story being built. `LORE.md` describes what is in the game today: as each
chapter ships, its text moves into `LORE.md`; when the last one ships, `LORE.md` becomes this story and this file goes.
Written by the director chat with Aleix (2026-11-13). Change it with him, then build.

Voice (unchanged): archaic, understated, melancholic, tender in places. No modern words, no jokes. Show, never tell
the whole.

## The spine

Act I is Ashmere as it stands: a nameless prisoner wakes in the Gaoler King's cells, rings the Bell of Waking, and
kills Aldric. But killing the king does not kill the Ember. It only frees the hunger that wore him. The hunger goes
back the way the Seven came, over the mountains, to the country they fled. The player follows it.

What Act II finds there is the thing Ashmere never knew about itself: **the Seven did not find the Ember under the
hill. They brought it.** Their homeland, **Thrale**, was eaten by it first. It could not be put out, so seven
households carried it away from their own people, sealed it under a hill in an empty land, and swore an oath that no
one of them would ever rule alone, because they had watched what it did to the one who did. Ashmere was built as a
lid.

Act III goes to where the Ember began, and to the one place in Thrale where fire never took. The hunger, beaten in
every body it wore, puts on the last one it kept: the champion's burned-out memory. The player fights who they were.

## The world

- **Ashmere** (Act I): unchanged from `LORE.md` sections 1 to 8: the Seven, the Oath of the Open Hand, the Bell of
  Waking, the Undying Ember, the Hollowed, the Ironbound, the Ashbound, the Toll Wisps, the Gargoyles, Aldric, Corvel.
- **The Pass**: the mountains between the two lands, and the log church where the Seven stopped and painted their own
  faces (`LORE.md` 10b).
- **Thrale** (Acts II and III): the old country. Once rich, gilded and proud; its kings wore the Ember one after
  another, each living longer and remembering less. Its people are **thralls**: alive, fed on, smiling. A storm has
  sat on its moors since the Seven left. Its capital, **Aurel**, is still gold.

## The player

The nameless prisoner of Act I, once **the King's Blade**, Aldric's champion, who would not burn Corvel and had their
memory burned out for it (`LORE.md` section 3). The burned memory did not vanish: the Ember ate it, and the hunger has
carried it ever since. Never stated outright; the red scarf and the round shield remain the only clue until the last
boss wears them too.

## People

- **Ysolde of Corvel** (the whole story): the bellwright's daughter, as in `LORE.md` section 5. After Aldric falls she
  follows the player over the pass, because her family's songs about the Seven name the place the hunger is going.
  She waits by the first bonfire of every chapter. In Act II she casts a small bell from what she finds; in Act III she
  rings it at the mere. The last line of the game is still hers: *someone is ringing something small*.
- **Finn** (Acts II and III, the solo companion): a deserter of the Ashbound, once the champion's squire, who was there
  when Corvel burned a year after the torch was laid down, and held a torch himself. He meets the player in the Pass
  and walks beside them through Thrale (the companion as built today: his own health and flasks, returns at
  checkpoints). Few words; he calls the player *ser* and never asks why they don't remember him.
- **Seren** is cut, with the whole old second road.

## The chapters

Each chapter keeps the shape the game has: a title and a line over the loading screen, Ysolde by the first bonfire, a
boss behind a fog gate, and one new enemy where the table names one. Act II and III levels are rebuilt from the old
second road's levels as raw material (layouts, terrain, weather, set pieces); every name, look and character from that
road goes.

### The chapter lines (drafts, one per loading screen)

1-5. As in the game today (`js/data/story.js`).
6. *The king is ash, and still the ash falls. Something left him as he died, and went up into the mountains.*
7. *Over the pass lies the country the Seven never named to their children. Its gate is still kept.*
8. *Two castellans hold Saltgrave for a crown that does not answer. They have held it a very long time.*
9. *Thrale too had a bell. The one who rang it was never let out.*
10. *Aurel is gold, and fed, and smiling. Its regent will be glad to see you.*
11. *A city that will not be kept is burned. You have seen this done before, though you do not remember it.*
12. *The monks of Thrale tried once to cut the hunger out of the earth. The earth came with it.*
13. *Under everything, the Ember is a root, and the root is still growing.*
14. *There is one water in Thrale that never burned. Carry it down. Something wearing your face is waiting.*

## The ending

1. The King's Blade kneels in the shallows. The scarf comes loose and floats.
2. The player wades out with the Ember and holds it under. The ice closes over it.
3. Over Thrale the storm breaks. Over the mountains, over Ashmere, the ash stops falling.
4. Finn stays at the water's edge. He does not say the name he knows.
5. Down by the river, someone is ringing something small.

Story mode's last screen: *The ash has stopped falling. The road is walked.* The credits name Ysolde and Finn under
*Who walked beside you*.

## Mysteries (hinted, never answered)

1. Who rang the Bell of Waking the first time, and which ruler came out? (as now)
2. Did Aldric's queen and boy truly die of the plague? (as now)
3. Did the Seven carry the Ember away to save Thrale, or to have it?
4. The Ringer of Thrale has a bellwright's hands.

## What goes

The old second road entire: its eight lands as named, Seren, its eight bosses and their arena category, its enemies
(Omenkin, Grafted Scion, Crucible Squire, Leyndell Knight, Ashen Warrior, Beastman, Golden Zealot, Cleanrot Knight),
its messages and tablets, its ending, and its lobby ceremony. Its levels, mechanics and boss engineering stay only as
raw material for chapters 7 to 14. Saves start fresh with the new story.
