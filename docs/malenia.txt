### Boss Attributes & Combat Parameters

* **Base Health:** 33,251 HP total (New Game)
* **Phase 1 (Blade of Miquella):** 18,473 HP
* **Phase 2 (Goddess of Rot):** 14,778 HP (resets to **80% max HP** after cutscene)


* **Defense:** 123
* **Stance / Poise:** 80 (breaks into a critical riposte window at her chest; regenerates after ~6 seconds without incoming stance damage).
* **Parry Resistance:** Requires **3 successful parries** to break stance and trigger a critical riposte opening.
* **Parry Vulnerability:**
* **Parriable:** Standard katana swings, lunging thrusts, and the majority of her grounded blade combinations.
* **Unparriable:** Waterfowl Dance (all 3 flurries), Lunging Grab, Counter-Kick, Scarlet Aeonia dive/blast, and all Phase 2 Phantom Spirit clone strikes.


* **Damage Types Dealt:**
* **Phase 1:** Standard, Slash, Pierce.
* **Phase 2:** Standard, Slash, Pierce, Holy, and Scarlet Rot buildup.


* **Base Absorptions / Negations:** Standard 10%, Slash 10%, Strike 10%, Pierce 10%, Magic 20%, Fire 0% (10% in water), Lightning 20% (10% in water), Holy 40%.
* **Status Resistances:**
* **Frostbite:** 306 (scaling: 348 / 446 / 736 / 1,193)
* **Hemorrhage (Bleed):** 421 (scaling: 519 / 809 / 1,266)
* **Sleep:** 689 (scaling: 979 / 1,436)
* **Poison / Scarlet Rot:** 1,481 (scaling: 1,938)
* **Madness / Death Blight:** Immune


* **Phase Transition:** Triggers when Phase 1 HP reaches **0**. Note that landing a riposte or critical hit that empties her health will not trigger Phase 2; she survives at 1 HP until hit with standard non-critical damage.

### Behavioral Triggers & AI Branching

* **Universal Lifesteal Mechanic:**
* Every attack that physically connects recovers a flat + percentage amount of Malenia's health (~1.6% to 6.4% of maximum HP per swing).
* Healing occurs on contact regardless of whether the hit connects with the player, hits a phantom/spirit summon, or is completely absorbed by a **100% physical damage-negation shield**.


* **Stagger Nullification & Hyper-Armor Overrides:**
* Malenia possesses low base poise (80) and standard flinch animations against medium/heavy weapon hits while in neutral.
* However, numerous offensive startups (e.g., Counter-Kick, Waterfowl Dance, Leaping Overhead, side-step repositioning) possess hyper-armor frames.
* If incoming stance damage reduces her poise meter to 0 while she is locked in hyper-armor, she cannot enter the stance-broken/riposte animation. Her stance damage resets immediately, skipping the standard 6-second stance recovery delay.


* **Ranged Projectile Input-Reading:**
* Firing an arrow, bolt, throwing knife, or targeted spell at medium-to-long range while Malenia is idle triggers an immediate lateral slide/dash to evade the projectile.


* **Consumable / Flask Input-Reading:**
* Initiating a flask or item animation at neutral range prompts an instant linear **Lunging Thrust** or a rapid dash-in sequence.


* **Waterfowl Dance Trigger Thresholds:**
* **Phase 1:** Becomes active once her health drops to **≤75% HP**. Typically follows an initial cooldown period before it can be queued again.
* **Phase 2:** Active at all health percentages with decreased cooldown between executions.

### Phase 2 Moveset & Additions (Malenia, Goddess of Rot)

At Phase 2, Malenia sheds her armor, gains butterfly wings, and begins at 80% HP. She becomes permanently aggressive, eliminating her Phase 1 passive walking states, and appends rot explosions to standard heavy attacks.

* **Scarlet Aeonia (Opening & Periodic)**
* *Tempo & Mechanics:* Hovers in the air, dives down violently into the arena floor, and detonates into a massive blooming scarlet lotus flower. The petal explosion inflicts rapid Scarlet Rot and continuous physical/holy damage for ~5 seconds.
* *Parryability:* **Unparriable**.
* *Punish Window:* **Major Window (Ranged / Melee at Conclusion):** Malenia remains completely motionless inside the translucent flower center. Once the petals begin wilting and the damage hitbox ceases, she is exposed to free charged attacks.


* **Phantom Spirits (Clone Flurry)**
* *Tempo & Mechanics:* Leaps high into the air surrounded by scarlet butterflies and channels 5 red phantom clones that descend in rapid succession:
1. *Phantom 1:* Direct vertical thrust.
2. *Phantom 2:* Sweeping slash.
3. *Phantom 3:* Upward slash.
4. *Phantom 4:* Jumping overhead slam.
5. *Phantom 5:* Thrust from the left.


* *Finisher:* Followed immediately by the real Malenia executing a lunging thrust from the air.


* *Parryability:* **Unparriable**.
* *Punish Window:* **Major Window:** Extended recovery immediately following the landing of the real Malenia's terminal thrust.


* **Rot Geyser Slams & Sweeps**
* *Tempo & Mechanics:* Jumping overhead slams and heavy sweeps now cause crimson rot geysers to erupt outward from the floor ~1 second after the blade contacts the earth.
* *Punish Window:* **Trap to Minor Window.** The primary sword strike recovery cannot be punished from the front due to the delayed secondary rot explosion; flanking behind her is required to punish safely.
