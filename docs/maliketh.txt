### Boss Attributes & Combat Parameters

* **Base Health:** 10,620 HP (New Game, Farum Azula)
* Health pool is continuous across both forms: Beast Clergyman (100%–50% HP) and Maliketh, the Black Blade (≤50% HP).


* **Stance / Poise:** 80 (stance breaks open a riposte window at his head; regenerates after ~6 seconds without incoming stance damage).
* **Parry Resistance & Mechanics:**
* **Phase 1 (Beast Clergyman):** Unparryable. Standard parrying tools cannot deflect Cinquedea slashes or Bestial Incantations.
* **Phase 2 (Maliketh):** Immune to standard shield/weapon parries. Specific aerial and sweeping sword attacks flash with a distinct **golden glow**; only these specific attacks can be parried using the tool **Blasphemous Claw**. A successful Blasphemous Claw deflection stuns Maliketh for **~4.5 seconds**.


* **Damage Types Dealt:**
* **Phase 1:** Standard, Strike, Pierce, and Physical magic (Bestial rocks).
* **Phase 2:** Standard, Slash, Pierce, Holy, and Destined Death.


* **Destined Death Mechanics (Phase 2):**
* All hits from the Black Blade or its crimson projectiles apply **Destined Death**:
* Reduces the target's maximum HP ceiling by **10%** for 15 seconds (resets on repeated hits).
* Applies an immediate damage-over-time (DoT) tick that drains approximately **2% of maximum HP** over several seconds.




* **Base Absorptions / Negations:**
* **Phase 1 (Beast Clergyman):** Standard 0%, Slash 10%, Strike 0%, Pierce 0%, Magic 20%, Fire 20%, Lightning 20%, Holy 40%.
* **Phase 2 (Maliketh):** Standard 20%, Slash 35%, Strike 20%, Pierce 20%, Magic 40%, Fire 40%, Lightning 40%, Holy 80%.


* **Status Resistances:**
* **Hemorrhage (Bleed):** 594 (susceptible)
* **Frostbite:** 594 (susceptible)
* **Poison / Scarlet Rot:** 594 (resistant)
* **Sleep:** 594
* **Madness / Death Blight:** Immune


* **Phase Transition:** Triggers when health drops to **≤50% HP** (initiates a cutscene; remaining HP does not regenerate).

### Behavioral Triggers & AI Branching

* **Consumable / Item Input-Reading:**
* In Phase 1, using an item or flask at medium-to-long range instantly prompts an untelegraphed **Bestial Sling** or a running Cinquedea swipe.


* **Dagger Retreat Cancel (Phase 1):**
* If the player lingers at point-blank range under the Clergyman's chest during a dagger combo, the AI frequently cancels the recovery into a leaping backward somersault while scattering **Bestial Sling** pebbles across the retreat path.


* **Pillar & Geometry Interaction (Phase 2):**
* Maliketh checks for nearby arena pillars when triggering aerial evasions. He can latch onto the side of a pillar mid-leap, push off vertically, and redirect projectile trajectories downward.


* **Golden Blade Flare (Blasphemous Claw Trigger):**
* During the windup of his aerial double-flip projectile follow-up and his leaping corkscrew dive, Maliketh's sword blade emits a bright golden particle flash. The active parry frame of the Blasphemous Claw must intersect with the weapon hitbox during this dive to force the stance break.

### Phase 2 Moveset & Additions (Maliketh, the Black Blade)

At ≤50% HP, the Beast Clergyman sheds his robes to reveal Maliketh, wielding the Destined Death Greatsword. Mobility, reach, and aerial tracking increase dramatically.

* **Black Blade Aerial Projectiles (Destined Death Waves)**
* *Tempo & Mechanics:* Leaps high into the air (or off an arena pillar), hovers momentarily, and flips, hurling 1 to 3 crimson/gold crescent energy waves toward the target.
* *Branching:* Almost always chains directly into either the **Leaping Corkscrew Dive** or the **Ground Slam into Destined Death Sphere**.
* *Punish Window:* **Zero Window.** Active aerial evasion; no melee punish possible during flight.


* **Leaping Corkscrew Dive (Gold Flash / Parry Window)**
* *Tempo & Mechanics:* Follows the aerial wave projectiles. Maliketh's blade flashes gold as he corkscrews forward through the air in a spinning downward dive.
* *Parryability:* **Blasphemous Claw Only.** Parry triggers when the golden flash descends into melee range.
* *Punish Window:*
* *On Parry:* **Major Window (4.5s Stun):** Maliketh falls to his knees, completely inert.
* *On Whiff/Roll:* **Minor Window:** Slides along the ground, recovering quickly.




* **Destined Death (AoE Slashing Sphere)**
* *Tempo & Mechanics:* Maliketh plants the tip of the Greatsword into the earth, hops onto the pommel with both feet, pauses, and backflips off. As he leaps away, a dense sphere of thousands of crimson slashes erupts from the blade, followed by an outward detonation.
* *Hitbox Risk:* Massive multi-hit blender dealing continuous Destined Death ticks.
* *Punish Window:* **Major Window (Position-Dependent):** The sphere only extends in front and slightly outward from the planted blade. Getting behind Maliketh during the pommel stand allows an uninterrupted punish.


* **Slow Overhead Two-Handed Slam & Ground Blast**
* *Tempo & Mechanics:* Rears back on hind legs, brings the massive blade high overhead with both hands, pauses with heavy tracking, and smashes it vertically down into the floor. A delayed holy explosion erupts along the line of impact after ~1 second.
* *Branching:* Can transition into a sweeping horizontal escape slash if the target is behind him.
* *Punish Window:* **Major Window:** Positioning to his lateral flanks avoids both the downward strike and the line explosion, leaving him static during the recovery.


* **Spinning Slash into Flip (Ground Sweep)**
* *Tempo & Mechanics:* Executes a low, wide-angle 360° sweep with the blade while sliding forward, ending with a vertical somersault backward.
* *Punish Window:* **Minor Window:** Safe to punish briefly if close to the landing point of his backflip.
