### Boss Attributes & Combat Parameters

* **Base Health:**
* **Stormhill Evergaol (Baseline):** 2,782 HP
* **Auriza Hero's Grave (Crucible Knight Ordovis):** 5,460 HP (NG)


* **Stance / Poise:** 80 (regenerates after ~6 seconds without incoming stance damage).
* **Parry Resistance:** **1 parry** immediately breaks posture and opens a riposte / critical hit window.
* **Critical Hit Modifier:** Takes **30% reduced damage** from ripostes / critical strikes compared to standard humanoid targets.
* **Parry Vulnerability:** All standard greatsword swings and stabs are parriable. Shield bashes, stomps, and Crucible Aspect incantations (Wings, Tail, Horns) are unparriable.
* **Damage Types Dealt:** Standard, Strike, Pierce, and Holy (Phase 2).
* **Base Absorptions / Negations:** Standard 35%, Slash 35%, Strike 35%, Pierce 35%, Magic 40%, Fire 20%, Lightning 20%, Holy 40%.
* **Status Resistances:**
* **Hemorrhage (Bleed):** Immune.
* **Madness:** Immune.
* **Poison:** 517 (scaling to 807 / 1,264 on successive procs).
* **Scarlet Rot:** 517 (scaling to 807 / 1,264 on successive procs).
* **Frostbite:** 517 (scaling to 807 / 1,264 on successive procs).
* **Sleep:** 517 (scaling to 807 / 1,264 on successive procs).


* **Phase Transition:** Triggers when health reaches **≤50% HP**.

### Behavioral Triggers & AI Branching

* **Consumable / Flask Input-Reading:**
* When a consumable or flask use animation begins at medium-to-long range while the knight is in a neutral or walking state, the AI immediately buffers a **Lunging Thrust** or a running diagonal sweep.


* **Shield Turtling & Deflection:**
* When approaching with the Greatshield raised, frontal light weapon strikes bounce off (deflection), immediately queuing a **Shield Bash** counter.


* **Phase 2 Tail Extensions:**
* In Phase 2, standard Phase 1 combo finishers (e.g., Two-Handed Downward Slam, Double Spin Slash, Lunging Thrust) do not enter recovery frames; the AI checks proximity to append an immediate **Aspect of the Crucible: Tail** sweep.

### Phase 2 Moveset & Additions (≤50% HP)

At ≤50% HP, the Crucible Knight briefly assumes an empowered state, manifesting golden wings and primal Crucible aspects.

* **Aspect of the Crucible: Wings (Flying Dive)**
* *Tempo & Mechanics:* Spawns golden ethereal wings, leaps ~15 feet vertically into the air while holding his greatsword pointed forward, hovers for ~45 frames, and dives in a linear trajectory toward the target.
* *Parryability:* **Unparriable**.
* *Punish Window:* **Major Window.** Upon landing, the knight slides forward on the ground and takes several recovery frames to stand and turn around.


* **Aspect of the Crucible: Tail (Single Tail Swipe)**
* *Tempo & Mechanics:* Conjures a golden dragon-like tail and executes a rapid 180°–360° horizontal sweep around his torso and rear. Very fast startup (~25 frames).
* *Branching:* Used dynamically as an untelegraphed combo finisher after any standard greatsword chain when the player is within close melee range.
* *Parryability:* **Unparriable** (can be jumped or rolled).
* *Punish Window:* **Minor Window.** Quick reset to neutral unless extended into the Double Sweep.


* **Aspect of the Crucible: Tail (Double Sweep / Charged)**
* *Tempo & Mechanics:* Immediately after the first tail swipe, the knight channels a second, significantly larger spectral tail that sweeps a massive radius (approx. 2.5x the reach of the first swipe).
* *Branching:* Triggered predominantly if the player attempts to roll backward or punish immediately after the first tail strike.
* *Parryability:* **Unparriable** (jumpable).
* *Punish Window:* **Major Window.** The knight is locked in place during the end of the second tail's despawn animation.
