### Boss Attributes & Combat Parameters

* **Base Health:** 10,399 HP (New Game)
* **Stance / Poise:** 80 (stance breaks open a riposte window at his chest/head; poise begins regenerating after ~6 seconds without incoming stance damage).
* **Parry Resistance:** Requires **3 successful parries** to trigger a stance break / critical riposte state.
* **Parry Vulnerability:**
* **Parriable:** Standard physical swings, thrusts, and running slashes delivered with the Cursed Greatsword.
* **Unparriable:** All spectral golden conjurations (Golden Spear, Erdsteel Daggers, Golden Great Hammer, Golden Curved Sword), Cursed Bloodflame blade skills, grabbing maneuvers, and rear tail sweeps.


* **Damage Types Dealt:** Standard, Slash, Pierce, Holy, Fire, and Hemorrhage (Bleed) buildup.
* **Base Absorptions / Negations:** Standard 10%, Slash 10%, Strike 10%, Pierce 10%, Magic 40%, Fire 40%, Lightning 40%, Holy 40%.
* **Status Resistances:**
* **Hemorrhage (Bleed):** 348 (susceptible)
* **Frostbite:** 348 (susceptible)
* **Poison:** 348 (susceptible)
* **Scarlet Rot:** 348 (susceptible)
* **Sleep:** 348
* **Madness / Death Blight:** Immune


* **Phase Transition:** Triggers when health drops to **≤60% HP**.

### Behavioral Triggers & AI Branching

* **Consumable / Item Input-Reading:**
* Triggering a flask or consumable animation at medium-to-long range while Morgott is in a neutral state causes him to instantly buffer an Erdsteel Dagger toss (single or double) or execute a leaping gap-closer.


* **Positional Traps & Off-Hand Extensions:**
* **Rear Flank (180° Arc):** Standing behind his hip axis immediately queues a fast, untelegraphed **Tail Swipe**, canceling the normal recovery animation of preceding attacks.
* **Off-Hand Lateral Proximity:** Lingering at Morgott's left side causes him to dynamically weave instant, untelegraphed golden dagger slashes into standard sword combo recoveries.


* **Distance-Dependent Spear Branching:**
* When Morgott conjures the **Golden Spear**, the AI evaluates target velocity and distance:
* *Player at Long Range / Moving Away:* Morgott halts, cocks his arm, and executes the **Spear Throw (Javelin)**.
* *Player at Mid Range / Stationary:* Morgott levels the spear horizontally and initiates the multi-hit **Running Spear Charge**.

### Phase 2 Moveset & Additions (≤60% HP)

At ≤60% HP, Morgott drops to his knees, begins coughing/choking, and detonates an arena-wide defilement burst. His sword cracks open, coating the blade in cursed bloodflame and unlocking geyser hazards across the floor.

* **Phase Transition: Defilement Burst**
* *Tempo & Mechanics:* Kneels down and channels cursed fluids (~3 seconds), then unleashes a heavy 360° explosive shockwave while erupting bloodflame geysers around the arena floor.
* *Parryability:* **Unparriable**.
* *Punish Window:* **Major Window (Ranged / Free Hits during Kneel):** Vulnerable while kneeling before the burst; must disengage prior to detonation.


* **Cursed Blood Slice (Bloodflame Dash & Detonation)**
* *Tempo & Mechanics:* Winds sword back as it ignites with red bloodflame, dashes forward with extreme speed, executes a horizontal sweep, and triggers a delayed secondary explosion along the strike path.
* *Parryability:* **Unparriable**.
* *Punish Window:* **Major Window:** Once the secondary bloodflame explosion detonates, Morgott has an extensive idle recovery state.


* **Bloody Thrust (Impaling Dash)**
* *Tempo & Mechanics:* Braces with sword pointed forward, dashes linearly at the target, and impales them. If it connects, the player is lifted, burned, and thrown down.
* *Parryability:* **Unparriable**.
* *Punish Window:* **Major Window:** Evading the linear path leaves Morgott exposed for the entire duration of the whiff recovery.


* **Arena Hazard: Blood Geysers**
* *Tempo & Mechanics:* Liquid pools across the floor begin bubbling with dark red fluids, erupting intermittently into vertical geysers dealing fire damage and heavy bleed buildup.
* *Punish Window:* Passive environmental hazard; restricts available footwork around Morgott during openings.
