### Boss Attributes & Combat Parameters

* **Base Health:** 13,347 HP (New Game)
* Precedes the Elden Beast encounter immediately upon defeat; health does not carry over.


* **Stance / Poise:** 120 (breaks into a critical riposte opening at his chest; poise regenerates after ~6 seconds without incoming stance damage).
* **Parry Resistance:** Requires **3 successful parries** to break stance and open a riposte window.
* **Parry Vulnerability:**
* **Parriable:** Standard physical swings, sweeping strikes, and lunging chops delivered with Marika's Hammer.
* **Unparriable:** Holy lightning projectiles, holy ground shockwaves, teleportation bursts, grab maneuvers, and the leaping Gold Breaker slam.


* **Damage Types Dealt:** Standard, Strike, Pierce, and Holy.
* **Base Absorptions / Negations:** Standard 10%, Slash 10%, **Strike -10%** (damage vulnerability), Pierce 10%, Magic 20%, Fire 0%, Lightning 20%, **Holy 80%** (extreme resistance).
* **Status Resistances:**
* **Hemorrhage (Bleed):** Immune.
* **Sleep:** Immune.
* **Madness / Death Blight:** Immune.
* **Frostbite:** 594 (susceptible).
* **Poison / Scarlet Rot:** 594 (susceptible).


* **Behavioral Threshold:** Begins incorporating **Gold Breaker**, **Elden Shattering (Tri-Slam)**, and rapid combat teleports primarily below **≤60% HP**.

---

### Behavioral Triggers & AI Branching

* **Spell & Projectile Deflection (Golden Retaliation):**
* When a ranged spell, incantation, or thrown projectile approaches while Radagon is idle or approaching, he raises his left palm. The projectile is neutralized instantly, immediately triggering an untelegraphed counter-cast of **5 homing golden bolts** fired in a tight spread.


* **Damaging Teleportation (Spatial Shift):**
* Radagon can execute instantaneous teleports both defensively (canceling out of hitstun/stagger) and offensively (closing distance).
* **Hitboxes:** Emits an active Holy/Physical damage hitbox at the departure point, followed immediately by a second damage hitbox upon reappearance at the arrival point.


* **Consumable / Item Input-Reading:**
* Using a flask or consumable at medium-to-long range prompts an immediate single or triple **Golden Lightning Spear** throw, or a forward teleport slam.


* **Lingering Rune Detonations:**
* Heavy hammer slams carve glowing golden sigils into the arena floor. These linger for ~1.5 to 2 seconds before violently detonating upward in holy pillars.

### Phase 2 Additions & High-Threat Attacks (≤60% HP)

* **Gold Breaker (Leaping Aerial Hammer Slam)**
* *Tempo & Mechanics:* Radagon leaps high into the air, hovers horizontally suspended for ~2 seconds while the Elden Ring sigil illuminates behind him, and drives the hammer straight down, generating an immense outward holy shockwave.
* *Parryability:* **Unparriable**.
* *Punish Window:* **Major Window.** Radagon remains kneeling on the ground with the hammer planted for ~1.5 seconds following the shockwave.


* **Elden Shattering (Triple Ground Slam / Tri-Slam)**
* *Tempo & Mechanics:* A fixed 3-stage slam sequence:
1. *Slam 1:* Slams hammer forward, projecting a narrow cone of golden shockwaves across the floor.
2. *Slam 2:* Swings hammer up and slams a second time, projecting a much wider 180° cone with increased delay.
3. *Slam 3:* Leaps vertically, pauses at peak height, and crashes down with full force, triggering an arena-wide 360° holy explosion.


* *Avoidance:* Slams 1 and 2 can be rolled through laterally or jumped; Slam 3 requires rolling inward with precise timing or clearing the radial line of sight.
* *Punish Window:* **Major Window.** Extended idle recovery strictly follows the detonation of the 3rd final slam. Intermediate pauses are frame traps.


* **Spear Thrust into Detonation**
* *Tempo & Mechanics:* Conjures a golden spear and drives it linearly into the ground, dealing direct pierce damage, followed ~1 second later by an explosive holy burst along the shaft.
* *Punish Window:* **Minor Window:** Accessible from his rear flank before the delayed spear explosion detonates.
