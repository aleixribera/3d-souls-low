### Boss Attributes & Combat Parameters

* **Base Health:** 6,080 HP (New Game)
* **Stance / Poise:** 105 (stance breaks trigger a riposte/critical hit window at his head)
* **Parry Resistance:** **Unparryable.** None of Godrick's melee axe swings or dragon attacks can be parried.
* **Damage Types Dealt:**
* **Phase 1:** Standard, Strike.
* **Phase 2:** Standard, Strike, Fire.


* **Base Absorptions / Negations:** Standard 10%, Strike 10%, Slash 10%, Pierce 10%, Magic 20%, Fire 20%, Lightning 20%, Holy 40%.
* **Status Resistances:** Poison 318, Scarlet Rot 318, Hemorrhage (Bleed) 318, Frostbite 318, Sleep 318.
* **Phase Transition:** Triggers when health reaches **≤60% HP**.

---

### Behavioral Triggers & AI Branching

* **Stormcaller Deflection:** The spinning wind cyclone active hitbox physically deflects standard physical arrows, bolts, and throwable pots.
* **Stormcaller Spacing Check:**
* **Player at Close/Medium Range:** Chains into a rolling reposition followed immediately by a **Leaping Overhead Slam**.
* **Player at Long Range:** Chains into **Storm Blade**, launching two curved, homing wind projectiles in succession.


* **Flank & Rear Counter (Two-Hit Chop Extension):** If the player circles behind Godrick during his primary axe chops, he can append a swift, backhanded swipe with his left grafted arm to clear the rear arc.
* **Repositioning Rolls:** Godrick executes ground rolls to the left, right, or rear. The roll has no damaging hitbox, but it cancels standard attack end-lag and immediately initiates his next offensive chain.

### Phase 2 Moveset & Additions (≤60% HP)

Godrick equips the grafted dragon head on his left arm, adding fire damage and altering several baseline attacks.

* **Opening Flame Sweep (Phase 2 Start)**
* *Pattern:* Immediately following the cutscene, Godrick braces and discharges a continuous horizontal jet of fire from right to left in a sweeping arc.
* *Punish Window:* **Major Window.** The frontal flame has extensive reach, but Godrick's flanks and rear are completely hitbox-free for the entire multi-second channeling duration.


* **Dragon Grab (Maw Scoop)**
* *Pattern:* Charges forward with the dragon's jaws agape scraping the floor. If it connects, the target is clamped in the maw, elevated, blasted with flame, and slammed to the floor.
* *Punish Window:* **Major Window.** If avoided or whiffed, the dragon head remains grounded while Godrick resets his posture.


* **Flaming "I Command Thee, Kneel!" (3 Hits)**
* *Pattern:* The two-hit earth slam is expanded into a 3-hit sequence: slam 1 (small radius) -> slam 2 (medium radius) -> slam 3 (massive area-wide explosion erupting upward).
* *Punish Window:* **Major Window.** Recovery frames only initiate following the 3rd eruption; intermediate pauses between slams 1, 2, and 3 are lethal damage windows.


* **Flaming Stormcaller & Flaming Storm Blade**
* *Pattern:* The wind aura is infused with flame. The leaping slam leaves behind fire on impact, and the Storm Blade follow-up fires two high-velocity spinning fire vortices.
* *Punish Window:* **Major Window** remains on the landing of the Leaping Slam; **Zero Window** during the cyclone startup.


* **Fire March (Advancing Flamethrower)**
* *Pattern:* Godrick steadily walks forward while aiming the dragon mouth downward, blanketing the ground ahead in fire before ending with an upward flame flick.
* *Punish Window:* **Minor to Major Window** depending on proximity—moving around his sides bypasses the frontal stream entirely.
