### Boss Attributes & Combat Parameters

* **Base Health:** 4,174 HP (New Game)
* **Stance / Poise:** 80 (regenerates after ~6 seconds without incoming stance damage)
* **Parry Resistance:** Requires **2 successful parries** to trigger a stance break / critical hit opening.
* **Parry Vulnerability:** Only wooden cane/staff melee strikes can be parried. Spectral golden weapons (Erdsteel Dagger, Golden Hammer, Carian-style Straight Sword) and tail swipes cannot be parried.
* **Damage Types Dealt:** Standard, Strike, Pierce, and Holy.
* **Phase Transition:** Triggers when health drops to **≤60% HP**.

---

### Behavioral Triggers & AI Branching

* **Consumable / Heal Input-Reading:** When the player initiates an item or flask animation at medium-to-long range while Margit is in a neutral state, he immediately triggers a single or double **Dagger Toss**.
* **Positional Proximity Branching:**
* **Rear Flank:** Standing directly behind Margit or at his rear flank triggers an immediate **Tail Swipe**, canceling his normal post-attack idle animation.
* **Left Flank:** Lingering close to Margit's off-hand side triggers a rapid, untelegraphed **Spectral Dagger Slash** as a combo extension.


* **Dynamic Overhead Tracking / Spacing Extension:** During the startup of the **Charged Overhead Cane Strike**, if the target retreats outside melee range before the swing descends, Margit aborts the stationary slam and transitions into a sprinting, gap-closing double horizontal sweep.

### Phase 2 Moveset & Additions (≤60% HP)

Phase 2 begins with the **Hammer Sweep and Leap Slam** sequence. Margit incorporates a golden great hammer and a straight sword into his existing repertoire.

* **Phase Transition: Hammer Sweep to Aerial Slam**
* *Pattern:* Materializes a golden hammer on his right shoulder, swings it horizontally in a wide arc, leaps high into the air, and slams it downward, creating an outward holy shockwave.
* *Punish Window:* **Major Window.** Margit is anchored in place during the hammer recovery animation.


* **Hammer Leap Slam (Standalone)**
* *Pattern:* Replaces the phase 1 cane leap. Margit jumps high into the air, tracks the player, and crashes down with the hammer, triggering an AoE holy shockwave on impact.
* *Punish Window:* **Major Window.** Guarantees combo termination with extended recovery lag.


* **Hammer Stomp & Overhead Slam**
* *Pattern:* Raises his left leg, winds the golden hammer overhead, and slams it directly down.
* *Punish Window:* **Major Window.** Functions strictly as a heavy combo finisher with zero branching follow-ups.


* **Staff-to-Hammer Spin Sweep**
* *Pattern:* Manifests the hammer on his shoulder, spins 360° clockwise, striking with the cane first and following immediately with the hammer.
* *Branching:* If the player backs away during the spin, he can chain this directly into a leaping horizontal sweep and ground thrust.
* *Punish Window:* **Minor Window** (if standing close and the leaping extension is not triggered).


* **Quintuple Sword Spin (Carian Flurry)**
* *Pattern:* Draws a spectral golden straight sword across his shoulder and performs an advancing sequence of up to 4 to 5 alternating spins between sword and cane, culminating in a downward strike.
* *Punish Window:* **Minor Window.** All swings must fully resolve before recovery frames begin. Intermediate pauses between spins are frame traps.


* **Sword Combo into Ground Stab**
* *Pattern:* Rapid thrust with spectral straight sword -> horizontal swipe -> ground staff plunge.
* *Punish Window:* **Minor Window** following the ground plunge recovery.
