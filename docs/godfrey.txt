### Boss Attributes & Combat Parameters

* **Base Health:** 21,903 HP (Leyndell, Capital of Ash — New Game)
* Health pool carries over between Phase 1 (Godfrey, First Elden Lord) and Phase 2 (Hoarah Loux, Warrior).


* **Stance / Poise:** 120 (breaks into a critical riposte window at his chest/head; regenerates after ~6 seconds without incoming stance damage).
* **Parry Resistance:** **Completely Unparryable.** None of Godfrey's axe strikes, stomps, grapples, or barehanded claw strikes can be deflected with standard parries.
* **Damage Types Dealt:**
* **Phase 1:** Standard, Strike.
* **Phase 2:** Standard, Strike, Slash.


* **Base Absorptions / Negations:**
* **Phase 1 (Godfrey):** Standard 10%, Slash 10%, Strike 10%, Pierce 0%, Magic 20%, Fire 20%, Lightning 0%, Holy 40%.
* **Phase 2 (Hoarah Loux):** Standard 0%, Slash -10%, Strike 0%, Pierce 0%, Magic 0%, Fire 0%, Lightning 0%, Holy 40%.


* **Status Resistances:**
* **Hemorrhage (Bleed):** 601 (scaling to 891 / 1,348)
* **Frostbite:** 601 (scaling to 891 / 1,348)
* **Poison:** 367 (scaling to 465 / 755 / 1,212)
* **Scarlet Rot:** 367 (scaling to 465 / 755 / 1,212)
* **Sleep:** 367
* **Madness / Death Blight:** Immune


* **Phase Transition:** Triggers when total health drops to **≤40% HP** (dealing 60% of his total health bar initiates the transformation cutscene into Hoarah Loux, Warrior).

### Behavioral Triggers & AI Branching

* **Ground-Level Hitbox Architecture (Vertical Clearance):**
* All foot stomp shockwaves (directional cones and arena-wide tremors) generate hitboxes strictly restricted to the ground plane. They possess negligible vertical height and are bypassed entirely via player jump states, allowing simultaneous airborne punish attacks.


* **Regal Roar Empowerment Thresholds:**
* **Phase 1 (≤75%–80% HP):** Godfrey channels his first **Regal Roar**. For the remainder of Phase 1, his standard directional stomps are permanently upgraded to 360° arena-wide shockwaves.
* **Phase 2 (≤20% HP):** Hoarah Loux enters **Hoarah Loux's Rage**, roaring with raised foot and stomping twice sequentially, upgrading his Phase 2 stomps to full-arena radial shockwaves.


* **Long-Range Gap Closing:**
* Retreating to medium-to-long distance causes Godfrey to prioritize either an advancing foot drag stomp or the **Jumping Axe Throw & Dive**, crossing the entire arena in ~1.2 seconds.


* **Phase 2 Command Grab Priority (Shield Bypass):**
* All grapple actions in Phase 2 (Power Bomb, Loux's Lunge) are unblockable. Guarding frames are pierced with 100% effectiveness.

### Phase 2 Moveset & Additions (Hoarah Loux, Warrior — ≤40% HP)

Godfrey kills Serosh and discards his armor and axe. Attacks transition to barehanded brawling, wrestling throws, and concussive shockwaves.

* **Power Bomb (Running Command Grab)**
* *Tempo & Mechanics:* Sprints forward with arms raised high, swings upward to scoop the target into the air, leaps into the stratosphere after them, and pile-drives them headfirst into the ground.
* *Blockability:* **Unblockable** (ignores shields entirely).
* *Punish Window:* **Major Window.** A successful dodge or whiff locks Hoarah Loux in an extended ground-recovery animation.


* **Loux's Lunge (Advancing Choke Grab)**
* *Tempo & Mechanics:* Lowers posture and charges horizontally forward with his right hand extended. Upon contact, throttles the target, raises them up, and slams them into the earth.
* *Blockability:* **Unblockable**.
* *Punish Window:* **Major Window.** Linear tracking; evading laterally leaves his back completely exposed for the duration of the slide.


* **Hoarah Loux's Earthshaker**
* *Tempo & Mechanics:* Unfurls a close-range concussive roar (staggers player), leaps forward, slams both fists into the ground (Shockwave 1), pauses, and pulls his arms outward, detonating a massive secondary radial explosion (Shockwave 2).
* *Avoidance:* Both shockwaves are ground-level hitboxes that can be jumped.
* *Punish Window:* **Major Window.** Substantial recovery lag follows the second radial ground eruption.


* **Berserker Flurry (Multi-Claw Swipes)**
* *Tempo & Mechanics:* Rapid, erratic advancing combo consisting of 4–5 alternating barehanded horizontal claw swipes, terminating with an upward cross-slash or front kick.
* *Punish Window:* **Minor Window.** Safe to punish only once the final cross-slash completes. Intermediate swipes have near-zero recovery.


* **Double Earth Stomp**
* *Tempo & Mechanics:* Stomps with his right leg (Shockwave 1), followed immediately by both fists smashing down into the floor (Shockwave 2).
* *Avoidance:* Jump both consecutive shockwaves.
* *Punish Window:* **Minor to Major Window** depending on spacing during the second fist impact.
