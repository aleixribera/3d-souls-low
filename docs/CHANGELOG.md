# Changelog

One line per release, newest first. Design notes for each feature: `docs/feature-history.md`; the current rules: `AGENTS.md`.

- **v7.28.0** (2026-11-12) — The Rampart Bowman built in Blender: the Hollowed archer (23 placements with the Castle Bowman) is now one rigid Blender piece per bone (a gaunt sunken face in a deep canvas cowl, a ragged shoulder cape, a fitted canvas doublet crossed by a leather harness, a back quiver of seven fletched arrows, bare sinewy arms with a studded bracer and glove on the bow hand and rag wraps on the draw arm, a doublet skirt, canvas tails, breeches, wrapped shins and turnshoes), 2,760 triangles with its bow; the iterate loop's notes start (`.claude/iterate.md`, `STATE.md`). Notes: `docs/bowman-model-v7.28.0.md`.
- **v7.27.0** (2026-11-12) — The Shield Warden built in Blender: the Ironbound gaoler (7 placements) is now one rigid Blender piece per bone (a tall flat-topped tower helm with a ridged face, a cross of slits and a riveted brow, a standing collar, a broad squared cuirass with a two-lame plackart, round pauldrons with lames, the sword arm's larger and spiked, mail sleeves, vambraces, gauntlets, a belt, mail skirt, fauld and a ring of gaol keys, mail under cuisses, knee cops, greaves and sabatons), 2,996 triangles with its kit-built greatshield and greatsword, down from 8,048; a new `dark` material slot; the Leyndell and Cleanrot Knights, which extend it, keep the kit's mail body, and validation now fails when a model's body is all null without baked parts. Notes: `docs/warden-model-v7.27.0.md`.
- **v7.26.0** (2026-11-12) — The Pale Duelist built in Blender: the Hollowed fencer (10 placements) is now one rigid Blender piece per bone (a gaunt face with sunken cheeks, a crimson beret with a white plume, a pleated millstone ruff, a peascod doublet with gilt buttons and a crimson baldric, paned sleeve caps and trunk hose, a doublet skirt of tabs, a two-piece half cape on its cloth bones, gauntlet cuffs and gloves, pale hose, tall boots with turned-down cuffs and gilt ankle straps), 2,665 triangles with its rapier and dagger, down from 4,197; a part's `.1` suffix names a bone's second piece in one slot. Notes: `docs/duelist-model-v7.26.0.md`.
- **v7.25.0** (2026-11-12) — The Rogue built in Blender: the Hollowed cutthroat (14 placements) is now one rigid Blender piece per bone (a loose hood with a swinging tail over a black face wrap with an eye slit, a leather jerkin open over blue-black scale, crossed chest belts and buckles, a back skirt, belt and pouch, front and back loin flaps, a leather pauldron on the sword arm, strapped bracers, gloves closed on the grips, a spiked off-hand bracer, knee cops and greaves over boots), 3,000 triangles with its scimitar and dagger, down from 5,248; a new `strap` material slot. Notes: `docs/rogue-model-v7.25.0.md`.
- **v7.24.0** (2026-11-12) — The Undead Knight built in Blender: the Ironbound heavy (11 placements) is now one rigid Blender piece per bone (a rounded close helm with a beaked visor, an eye slit and breathing holes, a gorget, a ridged breastplate, three-lamed pauldrons, mail sleeves, couters, vambraces, gauntlets closed on the grip, belt, fauld, mail skirt, tassets and knee cops over trousers, greaves and sabatons), 2,992 triangles with its greatsword, down from 7,388; `figure_lib.hard(outward=)` turns a one-sided sheet's faces outward; the model lab re-places the rig each frame, so models with the twitch idle (rogue, Pale Duelist) no longer drift out of view. Notes: `docs/knight-model-v7.24.0.md`.
- **v7.23.0** (2026-11-12) — The Ashen Lancer built in Blender: the second most common enemy (16 placements) is now one rigid Blender piece per bone (a skull with deep sockets in a pointed burlap hood, a poncho parted over the ribcage, a red cord, shoulder caps, bone arms, legs and feet with leather bindings, a hip wrap, waist tatters and a two-piece back cloak on the cloth bones), 2,992 triangles with its glaive, inside the enemy budget. Notes: `docs/lancer-model-v7.23.0.md`.
- **v7.22.0** (2026-11-12) — The Hollow Soldier built in Blender: the most common enemy is now one rigid Blender piece per bone (a quilted gambeson whose skirt is split into a back panel and a front half on each thigh, crossed straps, belt and pouch, puffed sleeves, cuffs and gloves, trousers, knee cops, turned-down boots, a crested morion over a sunken face), 2,920 triangles with its sword, inside the enemy budget; the Blender figure helpers are shared (`tools/blender/figure_lib.py`) and a figure's meshes can carry a name prefix (`hollow:`); the browser suite runs as four CI shards, and local test runs stay at idle priority. Notes: `docs/hollow-model-v7.22.0.md`.
- **v7.21.0** (2026-11-12) — The wanderer built in Blender: the player's model is now one rigid Blender piece per bone (hood and mantle, scarf, gambeson and cuirass, mail skirt and split coat, pauldrons, boots, a folded cape, the slung shield) instead of procedural primitives; null body slots no longer draw white limbs (the Giant Skeleton's arms and legs on Low and High); the baked packer writes one file per model set; a Changelog on the title lists every release for players (`js/data/changelog.js`). Notes: `docs/wanderer-model-v7.21.0.md`.
