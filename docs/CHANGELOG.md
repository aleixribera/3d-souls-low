- **v7.35.0** (2026-11-13) — The Calling, a co-op boss rush for a group of friends: the leader picks a roster of any bosses in the game (random or by hand), bots fill the party to three, one boss at a time with the last two together. Every boss scales its health, posture and breaks to the number of players (`CONFIG.group`) and fights the group, turning on the striker, a drinker or the closest and sweeping a bunched party (`CONFIG.aggro`). Between fights each player keeps one of three blessings for the run (`CONFIG.blessings`). The run ends on a screen with its time, bosses beaten and each player's damage and deaths, and Again for the same group. A risen player is no longer marked dead again by poses sent before the server's answer. Notes: `docs/feature-history.md`, the five Calling sections.
