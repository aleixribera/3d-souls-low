<!-- summary:start -->
Five reviewers, 67 findings (11 high); 42 pass after fixes, 25 left. What mattered most:

1. **The Summary was unreadable where it counts**: see-through on phone (name tags and the boss bar through its text), a strip in landscape, Notes below the fold. Now opaque, full height in short landscape, Notes second.
2. **Run state got lost in the header**: "Damaged" cut to "Da…" on phone, the version tag at 1.5:1 over the sky. Now the worst news comes first, one tag on small screens, a stronger scrim (7.8:1).
3. **Bodies without a readable owner**: the foreground knight in free camera was the downed Aleix, his tag floating at standing height. Downed tags are coral and sit on the body; tags stay inside the screen.

Left on purpose: a legend for timeline marks, a "behind live" readout, the landscape bar's height (39% of a 390 px screen), coral used for both live and down, and contrast at the edge of 4.5:1 for "Copy link" and the timeline fight numbers over bright sky. Not covered by the captures: a real phone's notch and keyboard, and a run longer than 4 minutes.

### F01 · On phone, runs on the same day look identical

- **Where:** list @ phone, box [20, 336, 350, 154] in [list@phone.png](2026-11-15/captures/list%40phone.png)
- **Rule:** NEW
- **Severity:** high · **Lens:** hierarchy
- **What:** The phone layout drops the time of day and the '2 notes' line, so the two 'Today' runs show exactly the same text. The player can't tell which run is which, and the one with notes can't be found except by the star. - **Fix:** Keep the time of day on phone: put it in the second line ('01:38 · Aleix, Marc') and keep the '2 notes' / 'deleted in 3 days' line.
- **After:** PASS: Phone rows now carry the start time, so same-day runs differ.
